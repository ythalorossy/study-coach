# Manage subscriptions

## Key concepts

- A Microsoft Customer Agreement (MCA) subscription is provisioned in your existing Microsoft Entra directory/tenant; you add more subscriptions to stay under subscription quota limits, to separate environments for security, or to isolate data for compliance reasons. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Creating one requires Owner or Contributor on the invoice section, billing profile or billing account, or the Azure subscription creator role on the invoice section. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- The wizard runs Basics (name, billing account, billing profile, invoice section, plan), Advanced (directory, management group, owners), Tags (name/value pairs), then Review + create, which must show Validation passed before Create. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Plan choice on Basics: Microsoft Azure Plan for DevTest for development or testing workloads, otherwise Microsoft Azure Plan. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Subscription owners on the Advanced tab must be users or service principals in the selected subscription directory; guest directory users cannot be selected, and a service principal is identified by its App ID. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- You can only pick management groups belonging to the current directory, and the subscription directory must be the Microsoft Entra ID where the subscription is created. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Azure resources — subscriptions, virtual machines, Azure web apps, Azure functions — cannot be provisioned in a Microsoft Entra B2B or Azure AD B2C tenant; use your Microsoft Entra tenant. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- If a brand-new subscription is missing from the list, a filter is usually responsible: open the Subscriptions filter, pick All, clear Show only subscriptions selected in the portal setting filter, then Apply. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Creating a subscription for someone in another tenant produces a subscription creation request: the customer gets an email with an acceptance link and has seven days to accept, otherwise the request expires; the requester can resend the ownership URL manually. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Subscriptions > View Requests lists pending creation requests for the source tenant's global administrator, the requester and the Subscription – Alias REST API caller; the target-tenant owner sees only the email, while anyone with request access can copy the Accept ownership URL. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- A Microsoft Online Services Program (MOSP) billing account can no longer add Azure subscriptions at https://signup.azure.com/. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))
- Turn on Azure Service Health alerting on a new subscription: it is free and recommended for production subscriptions. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/create-subscription))

## Settings and limits

- Cancel rights are identical across the listed types — Azure Free Account, pay-as-you-go, Visual Studio subscriber sign-ups, Microsoft Enterprise Agreement, Enterprise Dev/Test, Azure plan and Azure plan for Dev/Test all require a subscription owner without a condition. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- An account administrator who is not a subscription owner without a condition cannot cancel a subscription, which is the usual reason the Cancel option is missing from the portal. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Billing stops the moment you cancel, but the cancellation can take up to 10 minutes to appear in the portal, and services are disabled: virtual machines are deallocated, temporary IP addresses are freed and storage becomes read-only. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- After you cancel your last subscription the current billing cycle closes within 72 hours (three calendar days), so the final invoice arrives at the end of that cycle rather than on the cancellation day. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Cancelling an Azure support plan bills you for the remainder of the month and never produces a prorated refund. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- The Delete subscription option unlocks 3 days after cancellation for customer-led subscriptions and 7 days for field and partner channel subscriptions, and the Subscription Owner role is required to see and use Delete. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- The subscription is deleted automatically 90 days after cancellation, and Microsoft keeps your data for 30 to 90 days before permanently removing it, without charging for that retention. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- The Delete your subscription page gates deletion on two conditions: Delete resources (use View resources if it isn't green, and wait about 10 minutes after resource deletion for the status to refresh) and Manual deletion date (wait out the required period). ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Pay-as-you-go subscriptions cancelled by mistake can be reactivated in the portal; for anything else contact support within 90 days, and support's subscription-removal process allows only seven days to reactivate. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Before cancelling: back up data, stop all services from All resources, delete all resources and resource groups, and strip the subscription from AssignableScopes in custom roles, because updating such a role after cancellation can fail. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Manually cancel SaaS subscriptions before cancelling the Azure subscription — only pay-as-you-go SaaS subscriptions are cancelled automatically by the Azure cancellation process. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Free egress for customers leaving Azure requires an Azure Support request with your Subscription ID or Enrollment ID, the planned transfer start date and an estimated data volume; you then have 60 days from that date to egress (180 days for a UK billing address moving data from UK datacenters). ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- You must cancel all subscriptions after the data is out to request an invoice-level egress credit, which covers at most 60 calendar days of egress (180 for the UK case), excluding ExpressRoute, ExpressRoute Direct, VPN, Azure Front Door and CDN charges. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- For MOSP (pay-as-you-go) billing ownership transfer, only the account administrator of the account holding the subscription can transfer it, and you sign in to the Azure portal as that administrator. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Only one transfer request can be active at a time, it stays valid for 15 days and then expires; the sender cancels it from the subscription's Transfer billing ownership page. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Transferable subscription types are limited to Pay-as-you-go (MS-AZR-0003P), Pay-as-you-go Dev/Test (MS-AZR-0023P), Visual Studio Enterprise (MS-AZR-0063P), Visual Studio Professional (MS-AZR-0059P), Visual Studio Test Professional (MS-AZR-0060P), Action Pack (MS-AZR-0025P), MSDN Platforms (MS-AZR-0062P) and Azure Plan (MS-AZR-0017G, only for products in accounts created during sign-up on the Azure website). ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Transferring an Azure plan subscription requires Owner or Contributor on the invoice section the subscription is billed to; without it you get an access denied error. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Selecting Move subscription tenant puts the subscription in the recipient account's Microsoft Entra tenant and permanently removes all Azure role assignments — only the user who accepts the transfer keeps management access; clearing the option moves billing ownership only and preserves existing role assignments. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- The recipient accepts through the emailed link, then selects a payment method (signing up for an Azure account first if needed); sending or accepting a transfer request means agreeing to the transfer terms and conditions. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Visual Studio and Microsoft Cloud Partner Program subscriptions carry monthly recurring Azure credit that does not follow the subscription: after a transfer the subscription draws on credit in the destination billing account, and that credit resets every ninth day of the month. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))

## Compare and choose

- Goal is only to stop spending, not to close the subscription: delete all resources and resource groups to prevent unwanted charges instead of cancelling. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Support plan shared vs standalone: while cancelling a subscription, tick Turn off auto-renew only when no other subscription uses that plan, otherwise clear the option; for a standalone plan go to Cost Management + Billing Overview > All Billing Subscription > open the plan > Cancel > set auto-renew to Off. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Running resources present or absent: the cancellation page only offers Turn off resources when resources exist, and shows the support plan only when one is attached — you must tick Turn off resources to proceed with running resources. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Keeping the billing account but changing offer type: switch the subscription to another offer; to change who can reach the resources, change Azure built-in roles instead of transferring. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Transferring only billing versus also moving the tenant: pick Move subscription tenant when the subscription must live in the new account's tenant and you accept losing all role assignments; clear it to keep existing role assignments, and afterwards re-check role assignments, credentials (management certificates, storage access keys, VM remote access credentials) and the partner ID. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Customer type decides the transfer route: Enterprise Agreement customers have their enterprise administrator transfer subscriptions between accounts, while the self-service Transfer billing ownership flow covers MOSP/pay-as-you-go. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Before any transfer, confirm the transfer type is supported in the Azure subscription and reservation transfer hub — a missing Transfer subscription option means self-service transfer isn't available for that billing account, and subscriptions can't be transferred into a Microsoft Entra B2B or Azure AD B2C tenant. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))

## Gotchas

- Cancelling does not delete data or the account: data is kept temporarily at no charge in case you reactivate, Azure never auto-deletes your Azure account, and account deletion is separate (your Entra administrator removes it, or you remove the credit card). ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Outstanding uninvoiced charges and unapplied credits appear as estimates on the cancellation screen — the portal's example shows credits applied with pending charges and an estimated final bill of USD 50.50; the real invoice comes later. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Deleted resources may be invisible: if the page still reports active resources, open the subscription's Resources page, choose Manage view and Show hidden types, then delete them — active hidden resources block deletion. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- A subscription with disabled Access to Azure Active Directory is deleted automatically 90 days after cancellation; no manual deletion is needed. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Partners may suspend or cancel a subscription on customer request or for nonpayment or fraud. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Cancelling a Visual Studio Professional account means cancelling and deleting its Visual Studio Azure subscriptions as well. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
- Offers marked in the transfer table with a credit note (Visual Studio Enterprise, Visual Studio Professional, Action Pack, MSDN Platforms, Visual Studio Test Professional) leave any remaining credit behind — it isn't available in the new account after transfer. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- After a cross-tenant transfer with Move subscription tenant, only the accepting user can manage the resources; the new owner must manually re-add every user, group or service principal that lost access. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/billing-subscription-transfer))
- Egress credit is granted only if you follow the documented process — skipping the advance notice request or cancelling subscriptions before the data is out can make Azure refuse the credit, and the policy may change later; partner-purchased customers go through the partner. ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cost-management-billing/manage/cancel-azure-subscription))
